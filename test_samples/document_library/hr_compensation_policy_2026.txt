HR Compensation Policy 2026

Owner: People Operations
Classification: Confidential

Salary Bands
- Associate Engineer: INR 7.5L - 11.0L
- Senior Engineer: INR 16.0L - 25.0L
- Product Manager: INR 18.0L - 28.0L

Performance Revision Cycle
- Mid-year correction window: July
- Annual cycle: April
- Promotion calibration panel required for all grade changes

Confidential Clauses
- Individual salary details are restricted to HR and approved leadership.
- Compensation data exports require security logging and approval audit.
