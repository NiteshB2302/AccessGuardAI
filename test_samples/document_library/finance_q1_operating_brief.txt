Finance Division Operating Brief - Q1 FY2026

Prepared by: Corporate Finance Planning Office
Classification: Confidential

1. Revenue Snapshot
- Total recognized revenue: INR 48.7 Cr
- Subscription channel: INR 31.2 Cr
- Services channel: INR 17.5 Cr

2. Cost and Margin Notes
- Infrastructure expense increased by 8.2% due to regional expansion.
- Overall gross margin remained stable at 61.4%.
- Procurement renegotiation projected to reduce hosting spend by 5% in Q2.

3. Risk Watch Items
- Delayed enterprise collections from two strategic accounts.
- Foreign exchange exposure for EU vendor contracts.

4. Recommended Actions
- Weekly receivable monitoring for top ten customer accounts.
- Freeze non-critical discretionary spending through May.
- Increase controls on payment approval workflows above INR 15 Lakh.
