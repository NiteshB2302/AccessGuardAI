Operations Disaster Recovery Runbook

Owner: Operations Reliability Team
Classification: Internal

Incident Severity Matrix
- SEV-1: customer-impacting outage > 15 minutes
- SEV-2: degraded service with active workaround

Recovery Sequence
1. Activate incident commander and notify stakeholders.
2. Validate data integrity snapshots from primary cluster.
3. Route traffic to disaster recovery region.
4. Confirm API health and document portal accessibility.
5. Publish status summary every 20 minutes until closure.

Post-Incident Requirements
- Complete root cause analysis within 48 hours.
- File remediation backlog tickets with owner and due date.
